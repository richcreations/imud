imud

A general-purpose IMU daemon for Linux — “gpsd for IMUs”.

imud owns an I²C inertial sensor, fuses it with a quaternion MEKF, and publishes attitude, heading, rate of turn, heave, and sea-state to NMEA 0183, a binary UDP stream, and a local socket — for chartplotters, robotics, gimbals, and dashboards alike.

Prebuilt packages for Raspberry Pi OS and Debian are in the apt repository.
